# METACOMMMGMT
## Meta Community Management Professional Certificate
### Professional Certificate - 4 course series

This beginner-friendly professional certificate in community management, developed in partnership with Meta, helps you plan, grow, and manage online communities using Meta technologies, analytics and AI tools.

Community management is a growing digital skill used across marketing, social media, and engagement-focused roles. As brands turn to online communities to build stronger relationships with audiences, these skills are more valuable than ever.

The program focuses on real-world skills used by community managers, social media managers, and digital engagement specialists. You'll learn to develop engagement strategies aligned with business goals, create content that drives participation, establish community guidelines, moderate responsibly and measure performance using analytics and insights.

You'll gain experience with Meta AI tools, explore Meta products and apply your skills to real-world scenarios. The program also covers inclusivity, conflict resolution, crisis response, and long-term community growth, and prepares you for the Meta Certified Community Management Exam offered in the following languages: English, Deutsch, Português, Français, & Español.

This program is ideal for career starters, career switchers, small business owners, and professionals expanding their toolkit. No experience required. You'll finish with a strong foundation in community management, confidence to apply these skills across roles and industries, and a professional certification from Meta.
